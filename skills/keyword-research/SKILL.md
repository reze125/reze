# Keyword Research

## Tools
- Ahrefs / Semrush (유료)
- Ubersuggest (무료)
- Google Keyword Planner
- Also Asked (질문형)

## Metrics
- Search Volume: 월간 검색량
- KD (Keyword Difficulty): 랭킹 난이도
- CPC: 광고 클릭 단가 (상업적 가치)
- Intent: Informational / Commercial / Transactional

## Target Keywords (AI Tools Lab)
| Keyword | Volume | KD | Intent |
|---------|--------|-------|--------|
| chatgpt vs claude | 50K | Medium | Commercial |
| best ai writing tools | 20K | High | Commercial |
| jasper review | 5K | Medium | Commercial |

## Content Mapping
- High Volume + High KD → Cornerstone content
- Medium Volume + Low KD → Quick wins
- Long-tail → FAQ, blog posts
